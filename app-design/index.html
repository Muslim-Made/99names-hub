An app you open to feel the light.

Nineteen screens. The background is never chosen — it's the hour, read from the local prayer times. One name at a time, a dot that breathes, a quiet "do you remember?" on Fridays. No streaks, no red badges, no guilt. Use the hour dots above to see how the sky-following screens change through the day.

  • Arabic: Scheherazade New 600, line-height 1.75–1.9, side padding .2em. Its ink exceeds the em box; never sit Latin directly under it without ≥ .1em margin.
  • Labels: 9.5–10px, 0.24em tracking, uppercase, 500.

Shape & space
  • Cards 22 · sheets 28 · chips 14 · pills 999
  • 4pt grid: 4 · 8 · 12 · 16 · 20 · 24 · 32 · 40 · 48
  • Screen padding 22pt; safe-area aware.
  • Card ratio always 5:7. Shadow 0 30 60 -30 rgba(23,26,38,.5).
  • Surfaces: white at 55–60% on light hours, 10–14% on dark.
Motion
  • Ease cubic-bezier(.2,.8,.2,1) · fast .25s · base .5s · slow .9s
  • The breath: 4s in/out, scale 1→1.8, opacity .85→1. The one loop allowed on screen.
  • Screens rise 24pt + fade on entry. Cards lift 6pt on press. Envelope flap .55s, card out 1s.
  • Reduced-motion: all of it off except the breath at half amplitude.
  • Haptics: light on picks, medium on 33 / 99, none on errors.
Voice
  • Present tense, second person, gentle. "It will come back around."
  • Never: streak, test, score, missed, fail, unlock, badge, premium.
  • Always: remember, meet, met, close, gently, whenever.
  • Names are written As-Sabur (hyphen, capitalised article). The Prophet's name carries ﷺ.

Rules that make it #1.

The first thing on screen is a name, not a feature. Every screen, including onboarding and the store listing, leads with Arabic.

Nothing counts against you. No streaks, no "missed days", no red. Progress is dots that fill; absence is invisible.

One animation per screen. The breath. Everything else is a transition, and transitions are slow (.5–.9s) and eased the same way.

The hour does the theming. There is no dark mode toggle; Maghrib and Isha are the dark mode. Widgets and notifications follow the same clock.

Offline first. All 99 names, meanings, verses and audio are bundled. The app is complete on a plane.

No account. Progress lives on the device and backs up to iCloud / Google Drive. Send-a-Name works without login; payment happens in a web sheet.

Notifications are a gift, not a hook. One per day at most, each one a reflection line. Never "we miss you".

Arabic breathes. Line-height ≥ 1.75, padding on both sides, Latin never touches it. If it's tight, the Arabic gets smaller, not the space.

Every screen has an exit. "Not today", "No reminders", "Back to the path" are always full-size, never grey links.

Share, don't compete. The only social feature is sending a name to someone. No friends list, no leaderboard.
