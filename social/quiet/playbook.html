What Instagram
rewards now.

The plan for 5 October to 13 December, and the reasoning under every choice in it. Instagram in late 2026 ranks on three things above all: whether people send a post to someone, how long they watch, and how many like it relative to how many saw it. Reels are still the only real route to people who don't follow you; carousels earn the most saves and the most engagement per view; single images have lost almost half their engagement in a year. So the quiet months post four times a week: one name carousel to save, one reel to reach, one Jumu'ah carousel to send, one set to send to one person. Open the calendar.

The ten rules this plan is built on.

Each one is dated, because Instagram changes every quarter and a 2023 rule repeated in a 2026 blog is still a 2023 rule.

01

Sends per reach is the signal that travels.

Mosseri has named watch time, likes per reach and sends per reach as the top three. Sends weigh most with people who don't follow you yet.

Every caption ends with a reason to send it to one specific person. Sets are written for a named situation: a tight chest, someone far away, exam season.
Mosseri, May 2024 and Jan 2025; restated Sept 2026 · Social Media Today
02

The first second decides the reel.

Average reel watch time is 8.5 seconds. Instagram's own reels guide (28 Sept 2026) says engaging from the first second, with 15 to 60 seconds as the sweet spot.

Every reel's hook is already on screen at frame zero. Reels run 18 to 26 seconds and loop cleanly.
Metricool study of 24.4M posts, June 2026 · Instagram reels guide
03

Original work only.

Since 30 April 2026, accounts that mostly repost are cut from recommendations, now for photos and carousels too.

Everything is drawn by us: the ring, the cards, the envelope, the sky. No reposted calligraphy, no stock.
TechCrunch, 30 Apr 2026
04

Carousels are the save engine.

Carousels get 6.9% engagement per reach against 4.4% for images and 3.3% for reels, and about nine times the saves of a single image. Instagram re-serves a carousel at slide two to anyone who didn't swipe.

The weekly name is six slides. Slide two always stands on its own, so the re-serve lands.
Buffer, Mar 2026 · Metricool 2026 · Mosseri, Oct 2024
05

Reels reach; carousels hold.

Reels get 1.36 times the reach of carousels and 2.25 times that of images. Reels that are mostly text, silent or watermarked are shown less.

Thirteen reels in ten weeks, every one built on drawn motion with our own sound. The words support the picture; they never are the picture.
Buffer 2026 · Instagram ranking explained
06

Five hashtags, and the keyword first.

Instagram capped hashtags at five on 18 Dec 2025. Search now reads captions, on-screen text and alt text, and Google indexes public professional posts.

Each caption opens with what someone would search for, “Al-Karim meaning: The Most Generous”, then five tags. Every image has written alt text.
@creators, 18 Dec 2025
07

Three to five posts a week, steadily.

Accounts posting 3 to 5 times a week grow followers at more than twice the rate of those posting once or twice; weeks with nothing hurt. Most of a post's views come in its first three days.

Four a week, a day apart: Monday, Wednesday, Friday, Sunday, from 5 October. No empty week in ten.
Buffer, 2M posts, Aug 2025
08

Stories hold the people who follow.

Stories don't bring new reach, and resharing your own post there changes nothing (Mosseri, Apr 2026). They do build the ties that make your posts show up: replies, taps and sticker answers. Story replies rose 88% this year.

Stories six days a week. One-tap stickers (poll, quiz, slider, reveal) do the asking, because a small audience answers a tap long before it types.
Social Media Today, Apr 2026
09

DMs are the strongest tie.

The Reveal sticker hides a story until the viewer sends a DM. Every reveal is a DM thread that didn't exist before.

Every Sunday night, next week's name is a Reveal: “DM us to see tomorrow's name.”
Instagram stickers, May 2024 · Instagram
10

Topics, not just followers.

“Your Algorithm” lets people choose topics for Reels, Explore and now Feed (June 2026). Content that sits clearly in one topic is easier to recommend.

One topic, said the same way every time: the 99 Names of Allah, Qur'an, du'a. No drifting into general self-help.
Instagram, Dec 2025 to Jun 2026

No word on a drawn thing.

Every sheet is a page of an almanac: a running head, a body laid on a grid, and a foot carrying the line of ninety-nine. The rays, cards, envelopes, dots and the hour dial each get a cell of their own, and no word is laid over them. This is checked, not hoped for: check.mjs rasterises every sheet twice with the words hidden, once as drawn and once with every drawn thing hidden too, and the two pictures must match under every line of text. All 253 sheets pass. Every reel frame is checked the same way, against the shapes it declares.

words3:4 grid crop
Feed and carousel, 1080 × 1350. The profile grid crops to 3:4, cutting 34 px from each side. Every word sits 84 px in. The API refuses true 3:4 uploads, so 4:5 it is.
wordsreply bar
Story, 1080 × 1920. Nothing above 250 px (the progress bar and name) or below 340 px from the bottom (the reply bar). Sticker zones are left empty, ready for the real sticker.
wordsrailcaption
Reel, 1080 × 1920. Words stay in x 84 to 936 and y 250 to 1500, clear of the like-comment-share rail and the caption. The cover keeps its words inside the centre 3:4 the grid shows.

Stickers: what you do by hand.

No scheduler can place a sticker: Instagram's publishing API has no stickers at all, and the web version can't add them either. So the 51 sticker stories arrive in Metricool as drafts at their exact time, with an empty zone drawn where the sticker goes. The planner spells out every sticker's text and options for that day.

  1. At the time, open the draft in the Metricool app and choose to publish it manually. It hands the image to Instagram.
  2. Add the sticker over the empty zone and type what the planner says. A poll gets no question of its own, because the question is already drawn above it.
  3. Share. About thirty seconds a story.

Quiz: the quiz sticker has been unreliable on some phones since March 2026. If it's missing, use a poll with the first two options. Reveal: the story is blurred until someone sends a DM; reply to as many as you can, because each reply strengthens that tie. Link: point it to 99names.net/send with the text “Send a name”.

Loading it into Metricool.

  1. Plan. Starter (€20 a month, or €16 a month billed annually). Free stops at 20 posts a month, and this season is 150 items. The API needs Advanced; you don't.
  2. Account. @official99names must be a Business account, not Creator: only Business accounts can auto-publish stories. Connect Instagram through Facebook so licensed reel audio and your own Best Times work.
  3. Timezone. Set the brand's timezone to Africa/Lagos. The CSV has no timezone column; every time in it is WAT.
  4. Import. Planner → Import → CSV. Load metricool-01.csv, then the rest in order (50 rows or fewer each, as Metricool recommends). Date format YYYY-MM-DD, time HH:MM:SS. Check the preview, then the Feed Preview.
  5. The header. The column names come from Metricool's help article. Its own template sits behind a login, so download it once and run node schedule.mjs --template your-template.csv; every value is then placed by column name.
  6. Media. Every image is an sRGB JPEG and every reel H.264 with AAC at 128k and 48 kHz, which is what the Graph API and Metricool accept. The files are served from one public path on the hub, /social/quiet/out/media/, so Metricool can fetch them. Leave them there until the season has published.
  7. Optional: let Claude drive it. Metricool has an official MCP server (https://ai.metricool.com/mcp, any plan). Connect it and a session can create, move and check scheduled posts directly, for example “move Wednesday's reel to 20:00”.

What we skip, on purpose.

No sale, no countdown. Black Friday gets the one single image of the season: nothing is on sale. No crescents or lanterns, by the brand's rule, so Rajab is drawn as dots. No engagement bait: no “comment ameen”, no “type YES”. Meta demotes it and it isn't who we are; the stickers ask honest questions instead. No trending audio: every reel carries our own hour chords, which is original audio. If you want reach from a licensed track, Metricool can add one from Meta's cleared library at scheduling. No reposts of anyone's calligraphy, and no AI faces or people.

Honest risks.

Hijri dates. Every date here follows the Umm al-Qura calendar: 1 Jumada al-Akhirah on 11 November, the white days on 24 to 26 October and 23 to 25 November, 1 Rajab on Thursday 10 December. Nigeria follows the Sultan of Sokoto's sighting, which can differ by a day. The captions and stories say so.

Stickers cost time. 51 frames at about 30 seconds each is roughly 25 minutes across ten weeks. Skipping one is fine; the frame still works without it.

Trial Reels. Instagram can send a reel to non-followers first and share it with followers only if it performs. Metricool supports it, but your account has to be eligible. Check in the app, and if it is, try it on the Sunday dealt reels first.

Watch the numbers, not the rules. After four weeks, compare sends per reach and saves per reach by format in Metricool. If the dealt reels out-send the carousel sets, deal more of them.
