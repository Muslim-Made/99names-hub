Four a week,
at the pace of a name.

From 5 October the account posts four times a week: the name on Monday at dawn as a carousel to save, a reel on Wednesday at Maghrib to reach people who don't follow yet, Jumu'ah on Friday as three slides to send, and a set on Sunday at Asr to send to one person. Stories run six days a week; the ones with a poll, quiz, slider, reveal or link sticker are finished by hand on your phone. Every post wears the hour it goes up at, and no word is ever laid over a drawn thing. Pick a day in the calendar: the post is drawn at exact size with its caption, alt text and stories, and whether Metricool can publish it alone or it needs a hand.
