Pick the date. The post for that day is drawn below at exact size with its caption, its stories and its posting time. Download, copy the caption, post. Nothing needs editing. The month runs like a day: five posts at each hour, fajr to isha, so the profile grid reads as one gradient from night back to dawn. Every sheet is built to be sent to one person, because that is the signal Instagram now ranks on and because sending a name is the product. Templates for the weeks after live in the kit.

The month, at a glance.

A new account has one job in its first month: make a stranger understand what 99names is in one tile, then give them a reason to send it to someone. So the thirty days post daily, and the days are used in the order of a day. Fajr introduces the light and the mark. Morning shows how it works and what you hold. Dhuhr gives names for the heavy week and the quiet one. Asr teaches the hours and asks you to remember. Maghrib gives a name away. Isha closes with the patient ones and hands the account to the weekly rhythm: one name, every Monday, from the kit. Four reels, one a Wednesday, built from the mark, the breath, the sky and the envelope. Nothing is photographed; everything is drawn, the way the brand is.
