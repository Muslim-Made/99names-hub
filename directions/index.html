The decision

Direction 03 · Noor is the master brand, with Direction 02's 99-dot grid kept inside it as the progress and quiz device. Noor has the widest global appeal and the strongest retention mechanic — the page and the app tint themselves to the local prayer time — while the dot grid remains the most ownable single device across all three decks. Direction 01 stays available as the premium “Gilded” print edition within the range. The live site, the logo set and the full Noor world are all built on this system.
