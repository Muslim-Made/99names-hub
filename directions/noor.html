01 — Strategy

Position: the daily light.

Calm built a $2B company on "ten minutes of quiet." Muslims already have that practice — five times a day — and no brand that feels like it belongs to them. Noor positions 99names not as a reference, not as a challenge, but as a companion: the softest, most beautiful thing on your phone, and the card you keep on your bedside table.

I

Light is the system

Every name is a ray of the same light. The logo is 99 rays. Gradients move from dawn to dusk through the day. The brand literally changes colour with the prayer times.

II

Slow by design

Nothing is urgent. No streaks, no red badges, no "you missed a day." The app breathes — quite literally, a 4-second breathing dot on every screen.

III

Arabic that moves

Ruq'ah and Nastaʿlīq — the handwritten, flowing scripts — rather than formal Naskh or rigid Kufic. The Arabic feels like a note left for you, not a plaque.

Voice

Gentle. Present tense. Personal.

We speak like a good friend at dawn. "As-Sabur. The Patient. Take your time today — He does." · "Al-Wadud loves you in the mess, not after it." Always lowercase tone, even when the type isn't.

Tagline

"Light, by name."

Supporting: "One name, one dawn." (app) · "Keep a name close." (cards) · "Ninety-nine ways He's near." (campaign).

02 — Logo system

Ninety-nine rays.

A circle of 99 fine rays — count them — with a clear centre. The centre holds a name, a number, a photograph, or nothing. At small sizes the rays merge into a soft sun; at large sizes you can see each one. The wordmark is Fraunces, lowercase, with the "9"s set a touch wider so the numeral reads as a pair.

03 — Colour

The colour of the hour.

The palette is a day. Four dawn tints form the signature gradient; four saturated "hour" colours mark Fajr, Dhuhr, Asr/Maghrib and Isha. Ink is warm, never pure black. Night mode is navy, never grey.

Rule

Gradients are always the four dawn tints in order. Hour colours are used flat, one at a time, for a full-bleed moment. Ink #2A2622 for all text on light; Sand #F4EEE4 on night.

Print

Cards print on Fedrigoni Arena Natural with a 4-colour gradient + one spot (Pantone 7407 C for Fajr gold). No foil — the warmth is in the paper and the tint.

Contrast

Ink on Blush 11.2:1 · Ink on Sky 10.1:1 · Sand on Night 13.4:1. Never set body text on the Fajr or Rose tints smaller than 16px.

06 — App

It breathes.

The background is the time of day. Open at Fajr: blush. At Isha: night. One name sits in the middle of the screen with a slowly breathing dot beneath it. Quiz is there, but framed as "remember", not "test". Reminders are gradients, not alerts.

Icon

Dawn gradient with the 99-ray mark in ink. Reads as a sun on the home screen.

Motion

Everything eases over 600–900ms. Gradients drift. The breathing dot is 4s in, 4s out — the pace of slow dhikr.

Sound

Recitation by a single warm voice, recorded close. Optional soft room-tone bed. No chimes.

09 — Rules

Do / Don't

Do

Use the four-tint gradient, always in order.
Let things be quiet — a single name on a full screen is a complete post.
Use warm ink, never black.
Photograph real light: window, dawn, fabric, a card on a sill.

Don't

No black, no pure white, no gold foil.
No hard edges — everything has a radius.
No urgency language: never "don't miss", "streak", "last chance".
No stock mosque photography or bokeh lanterns.
