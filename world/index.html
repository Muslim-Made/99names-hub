06 — Why Noor wins

The honest case.

For

The colour-of-the-hour idea is a retention mechanic nobody in the category has — the app literally looks different at Fajr and Isha, which gives people a reason to open it twice. The 99-ray mark is ownable, scales from favicon to A2, and doesn't touch a single cliché. Send-a-Name is a $6 product with a built-in reason to share. And the tone matches where your Instagram is already going.

Against — and the answer

"It looks like a wellness app." It does, until the Arabic appears — which is why the Arabic is always large, always first, always Ruq'ah/Nastaʿlīq rather than a Latin-friendly Naskh. "Gradients are easy to copy." The gradient isn't the brand; the 99 rays, the hour system and the voice are. "Too soft for men / older buyers?" The Paper card (B) and the navy box exist for exactly that audience.

Next steps if this is the one

1 · Commission a Ruq'ah calligrapher for the 99 names (one-time, ~$1.5–3k, becomes your single biggest asset). 2 · Logo file set: rays mark + wordmark + ٩٩, SVG/PNG, icon sizes. 3 · Build the site on this system — home, 99 name pages, remember, shop. 4 · Print proof of card A and F with the paper + gradient spec.
