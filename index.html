The brand in public. The Quiet Months are the plan in use: four a week to Rajab, scheduled through Metricool. The First Thirty, the daily launch month, is archived. Behind them, thirty templates as plain HTML for every week after: click any text to edit it, re-tint the whole post to any of the six hours, pick any of the 99 names and the Arabic, the meaning and the palette refill.

The App

An app you open once a day and leave lighter. Twenty screens at phone size with the tokens and the rules beside them: today's name, the deck, remembering, sending, the hour dial. The Expo build follows this page screen for screen.

The designiOS · Android · Expo

٢٠The App Design

Every screen at 390 by 844, in five rows: arriving, today, the deck, remembering, sending. Tokens and rules alongside. Hover to leaf through.

How it is builtNo backend

The plan

Expo with expo-router and MMKV. No database, no accounts. The site is the only server, for checkout and for the Send-a-Name link. The core of names, moods and hours is generated from the site so the two never drift.
